R01: Financial services, regulation and ethics

A focused revision tool, not a bloated course platform.

It combines a full question bank with a spaced repetition system, timed mock exams, and clear explanations for every answer. The aim is not just to get you through more questions. It is to help you remember what you have covered, spot where your understanding is thin, and build the kind of recall that holds up under exam conditions.

Questions are introduced gradually, then brought back at increasing intervals as recall improves. That gives you a clearer sense of progress, and a more useful way to revise than endlessly starting again from the top.

How the review works

A correct answer pushes the question further out. A weak one brings it back sooner. The point is simple: to spend less time rereading material you half-recognise, and more time strengthening what is still fragile.

Why this exists

Built by someone who wanted a better way to revise.

I'm Michael Rice. I'm training as a financial adviser, and I built R0Ready because I wanted a revision tool that felt more serious than the usual options.

When I started studying, most of what I found fell into two camps: expensive courses that made the process bigger than it needed to be, or flat question banks that gave you no real help in remembering what you had covered. Plenty of material. Not much method.

R0Ready is my attempt to build something better: small, focused, and grounded in how memory actually works. Spaced repetition has been used for years in other demanding fields because it does something ordinary revision often fails to do. It helps knowledge stay put.

Before this I studied philosophy, taught abroad, and worked in edtech. I also write Unadvised, a newsletter about money, work, and career change.
