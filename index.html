Why this exists

I'm a career changer training to become a financial adviser. Before this, I studied philosophy, taught English in Thailand and Italy, worked in edtech, and spent a lot of time trying to figure out what I actually wanted to do.

When I started studying for R01, I found the revision process frustrating: dense material, limited practice questions, and no clear sense of whether what I'd studied last week had actually stuck. So I built the tool I wished existed.

R0Ready begins with R01, but the aim is broader: a family of CII revision tools that feel thought-through, useful and honest about what stage each module is at.
