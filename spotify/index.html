What it is for

This is the audio companion to the revision flow, not a separate product pretending to be a course. The episodes follow the same exams, the same modules, and the same basic idea: make the material easier to understand, easier to hold, and easier to come back to later.

  • One module at a time
  • One episode per topic
  • Built by someone taking the exams now

Why it exists

The question bank helps you find what you do not know. The podcast helps you hear it explained cleanly, out loud, by someone close enough to the exams to know where the confusion usually starts.

It is there for commutes, walks, and the parts of the week when staring at a textbook is not realistic but you still want to keep the material moving.
